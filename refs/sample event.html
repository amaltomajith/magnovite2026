PIXEL PERSPECTIVES 📸🏢

Capturing the built and unbuilt environment

Pixel Perspectives is a digital and photography contest inviting participants to explore narratives hidden within the built and unbuilt environment, capturing architecture beyond its functional role. The event encourages students and enthusiasts to frame architecture beyond its functional role, capturing overlooked spaces, fleeting moments, and striking interactions between form, light, and context. Both online and offline entries are welcomed, promoting creativity in visual storytelling.

Round Structure

Exploration Shot – Online

Round 1

Participants submit one photograph on either theme: "The Forgotten Corners" or "Framing the Sky". Each entry must be accompanied by a short caption/description (max 50 words).

On-the-Spot Capture – Offline

Round 2

Shortlisted participants will be given a surprise theme on campus. **Time limit: 2 hours** to capture and submit one photograph, which must be taken within campus during the event.

Rules & Regulations

Eligibility: Open to all students; team size of 2 members permitted.
Equipment: Any type of camera (DSLR, mobile, or digital) is permitted.
Editing: Edited/manipulated images will be disqualified (minor corrections like brightness/contrast allowed).
Originality: Entries must be original and owned by the participant; plagiarism will lead to disqualification.
Judging Criteria: Creativity, composition, theme relevance, and narrative quality.
Each institution can have a maximum of two registrations as contingents for every event
