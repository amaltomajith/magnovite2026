Welcome to Magnovite'25

MAGNOVITE is the annual flagship fest organised by CHRIST (Deemed To be University), Kengeri Campus. It is an enigmatic nexus of creativity and collaboration, bringing healthy competition and positive connections between participants. It returns this year with its 15th edition, bringing back its unfading charm.

Live Performance

Welcoming Masala Coffee to Magnovite 2025

This dynamic Indian band is celebrated for blending diverse genres—including Indian folk, blues, pop, and rock—into a unique and high-energy sound.

Since their founding in 2014, Masala Coffee has captivated audiences with original compositions and covers in multiple languages (Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, etc.). They are also recognized for their film music contributions to movies like Solo and Kannum Kannum Kollaiyadithaal.

Don't miss their genre-bending set on the main stage!

DJ Event

Witness DJ Rubz Live on the Main Stage

We are ecstatic to welcome the sensational DJ Rubz to our campus! As an official Sunburn-affiliated artist, DJ Rubz is a force to be reckoned with in the Indian music scene, renowned for his versatility and ability to keep any crowd engaged.

With years of experience tearing up stages across the nation, Rubz has perfected a high-energy set that fuses the best of Bollywood bangers with cutting-edge EDM, Hip-Hop, and Commercial hits.

His unique style isn't just about playing music; it's about curating an immersive experience—a journey of rhythm and light that leaves the audience breathless.
