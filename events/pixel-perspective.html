Pixel Perspective 📸🏢

Digital photography exploring architecture & form

📌 Event Overview

Digital photography challenge focusing on architectural aesthetics, light interaction, macro perspective, and visual storytelling across campus and urban settings.

TOTAL PRIZE POOL
🏆 ₹22,000
📅 Date 15-16 Sept 2026
💰 Registration Fee ₹300
👥 Team Size 2 Members
🎯 Format / Rounds 2 Rounds (Online Submission + On-the-Spot Capture)

Round Structure

Round 1: Exploration Shot (Online)

Submit one photograph on theme "The Forgotten Corners" or "Framing the Sky".

Round 2: On-the-Spot Capture (Offline)

2-hour sprint on campus following a surprise theme.

Rules & Guidelines

  • DSLR, mirrorless, or mobile cameras permitted.
  • Heavy manipulation/editing prohibited (basic exposure adjustments allowed).
  • EXIF data must be preserved in RAW/JPEG submissions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are mobile cameras eligible?

A: Yes, high-resolution mobile photos are eligible.
