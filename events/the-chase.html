The Chase

Campus-wide GPS treasure hunt & speed checkpoints

Event Overview

Campus-wide high-speed treasure hunt with real-time GPS clues, physical challenges, and speed checkpoints. Race against rival teams to claim the ultimate trophy.

Round Structure

Non-Stop Campus Sprint

Solve sequential GPS clues to reach 8 physical checkpoints.

Rules & Guidelines

  • All team members must cross checkpoints together.
  • No vehicles or motorized transport allowed.
  • Tampering with clue markers leads to immediate DQ.
