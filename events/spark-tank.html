Event Overview

Pitch startup innovations, business models, and working prototypes to seasoned venture capital mentors and angel investors. Prove market viability, unit economics, and scalability.

TOTAL PRIZE POOL
₹30,000
Date 16 Sept 2026
Registration Fee ₹400
Team Size 2-4 Members
Format / Rounds 2 Rounds (Elevator Pitch + Deep Dive Q&A)

Round Structure

Round 1: 3-Minute Elevator Pitch

Present high-level value proposition and market problem statement to preliminary judges.

Round 2: Boardroom VC Pitch

Full 10-slide deck presentation followed by intense investor Q&A.

Rules & Guidelines

  • Pitch deck maximum 10 slides.
  • Time limit: 6 minutes presentation + 4 minutes investor Q&A.
  • Working prototypes or wireframes earn bonus evaluation points.
