MAGNOVITE '26

MAGNOVITE is the premier 16th edition annual inter-collegiate fest of CHRIST University, Kengeri Campus, bringing together visionaries, creators, and competitors across India.

15,000+ Footfall & Participants
50+ Technical & Cultural Events
120+ Participating Colleges
₹5 Lakh+ Prize Pool & Awards

Our Legacy

Over 15 celebrated editions, MAGNOVITE has evolved into a hallmark event for student innovation. It fosters healthy competition, cross-disciplinary collaboration, and positive connections among students across engineering, management, architecture, and humanities.

From high-stakes hackathons to electrifying stage performances, every moment is crafted to challenge limits and inspire greatness.

The Campus Experience

Nestled amid lush green landscapes in Mysuru Road, Bengaluru, CHRIST University Kengeri Campus offers world-class infrastructure including state-of-the-art research labs, auditoriums, sports arenas, and vibrant amphitheaters.

It serves as the perfect canvas for two days of non-stop energy, learning, and celebration.
