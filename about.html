Our Legacy

Now in its 16th celebrated edition, MAGNOVITE has evolved into a hallmark event for student innovation. It fosters healthy competition, cross-disciplinary collaboration, and positive connections among students across engineering, management, architecture, and humanities.

From high-stakes hackathons to electrifying stage performances, every moment is crafted to challenge limits and inspire greatness.

The Campus Experience

Nestled amid lush green landscapes in Mysuru Road, Bengaluru, CHRIST University Kengeri Campus offers world-class infrastructure including state-of-the-art research labs, auditoriums, sports arenas, and vibrant amphitheaters.

It serves as the perfect canvas for two days of non-stop energy, learning, and celebration.
